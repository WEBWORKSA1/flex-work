# Competitive research (October 2026)

We fetched and reviewed 41 sites in flexible work and adjacent niches.

- **Remote job boards:** FlexJobs, We Work Remotely, Remote.co, Remotive, Remote OK, Working Nomads, Himalayas, JustRemote, Dynamite Jobs, Jobspresso, NoDesk, 4dayweek.io, Virtual Vocations, Remote Woman, Hubstaff Talent, Built In, Indeed (remote), Glassdoor (remote)
- **Freelance marketplaces:** Fiverr, Toptal, Contra, Freelancer.com, Wellfound, PowerToFly
- **EOR, HR and hiring software:** Remote.com, Deel, Oyster, Pebl (formerly Velocity Global), Workable
- **Research and thought leadership:** 4 Day Week Global, Buffer State of Remote Work, Owl Labs, Gallup, WFH Research, Zapier remote guide, GitLab all-remote handbook, Twist/Doist guides, HBR, The Muse
- **Nomad and travel:** Nomads.com, Remote Year

Five sites could not be fetched (Upwork, LinkedIn, flex.scoop.com, Pangian, the old Hubstaff Talent URL). Equivalent data came from primary reports instead.

## What we took from the research

| Pattern seen | How Flex.Work uses it |
|---|---|
| Filter by arrangement type (only 4dayweek.io does this, and only for 4-day variants) | Role explorer filters across all six arrangement types |
| Location-eligibility filters on every major board | Job alerts and employer forms capture location eligibility |
| Email job alerts with consent line (Indeed, Working Nomads) | Job alert form on the explorer |
| Preference onboarding wizard (4dayweek.io, Remote.co) | Three-step talent-network form |
| Intent picker leading to a demo (Deel) | Employer form, plan selector and tiers |
| Calculators as lead magnets (Oyster, Pebl) | Eight free tools, each ending in a lead CTA |
| Tiered paid listings (We Work Remotely, JustRemote $189, Remote OK) | Free, $149 Featured and custom Hiring Partner tiers |
| Ungated, citable stats (Buffer) | Stats band and research page, every figure linked to its source |
| Glossary (Deel), handbook-style guides (GitLab, Zapier) | Glossary page and 14 guides with tables of contents |
| Scam warnings and trust badges (FlexJobs, Working Nomads) | Scam guide; "employers are checked" promise |
| Company flexibility directory (Flex Index, now winding down) | Flex Employer Index submission form, to become a directory |
| Contests (Freelancer.com, Contra) | Contests page with rules and an entry form |

**Avoided on purpose:** paywalls, countdown timers, blurred listings and fake job postings. These hurt trust and conflict with AdSense quality guidelines.

Statistics and verified video IDs are in `_build/data.py`, each with its source.
