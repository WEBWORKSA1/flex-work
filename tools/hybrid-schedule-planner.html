Choosing an anchor day

An anchor day is when the whole team is in the office together. Put planning, workshops, one-to-ones and social time there, and leave focus work for home days. Most teams settle on one or two anchor days mid-week.

Gallup found in 2025 that only 11% of employees are on teams that set their hybrid policy together. Planning the schedule as a team — with this tool — is a simple way to fix that. Compare models in hybrid schedules compared.
