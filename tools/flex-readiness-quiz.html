How it’s scored

Each answer scores 0–3 across five areas that research and remote-first companies consistently link to success in flexible work: focus, written communication, home setup, social energy and routines. Your answers stay in your browser.

What your score means

80% and above — fully remote ready. You have the habits remote-first employers screen for. Say so explicitly in applications and interviews, with examples.

60–79% — hybrid sweet spot. Two or three home days with a predictable anchor day suits you. Work on your lowest area before going fully remote.

40–59% — flex-curious. Start with a trial: one home day, flexitime or a compressed fortnight. Build routines first.

Below 40% — structure first. You get energy from in-person structure. Part-time, shift choice or compressed hours may suit you better than remote work.

Improve your weakest area

  • Focus: time-block your calendar and close chat during focus blocks.
  • Communication: end every message with the ask and the deadline.
  • Setup: see our home office guide.
  • Social: schedule regular coworking or meetups.
  • Routine: use a fixed start and shutdown ritual.
