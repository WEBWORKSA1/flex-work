Flexible work request letter generator

Answer a few questions and get a clear, trial-based request your manager can say yes to.

Why this structure works

Managers approve requests when they can see the risk is handled. This letter leads with the arrangement and start date, explains the benefit to the team, shows how work and meetings will be covered, and proposes a reversible trial with a review. Read the full method in how to ask for flexible work.

Strengthen your case with numbers from the commute savings calculator and published research on our resources page. Everything you type stays in your browser.
