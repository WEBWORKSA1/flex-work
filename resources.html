Flexible work research and resources

The data behind flexible work, with links to every original source — free to cite in your own request, report or policy.

FindingSource
54% of remote-capable US employees work hybrid; 25% are fully remote.Gallup, Aug 2026
66% of US companies offer some location flexibility, with 2.87 required office days on average.Flex Index Q3 2025 (LSE)
35% of employed Americans did some or all of their work at home on days they worked.US BLS, American Time Use Survey 2025
37% of workers would reject a job that didn't offer flexible hours.Owl Labs State of Hybrid Work 2025
$61 spent per office day by hybrid workers, versus $19 on a home day.Owl Labs 2024
46% of remote-capable workers say they'd be unlikely to stay if remote work ended.Pew Research, Jan 2025
64M Americans freelanced in 2023 — 38% of the workforce.Upwork Freelance Forward 2023
89% of UK four-day-week pilot firms were still running it a year later.Autonomy pilot follow-up, via HRD Connect, Feb 2024
61% of remote-capable US employees prefer hybrid, 33% prefer fully remote and 6% prefer on-site.Gallup, Nov 2025
Only 11% of employees are on teams that set their hybrid policy together.Gallup, May 2025
About a quarter of paid US workdays were worked from home in late 2025.WFH Research (SWAA), Dec 2025
Workers say they would accept a 9% pay cut for flexible hours and 8% for a four-day week.Owl Labs 2025
40% of US workers were fully or partly remote in 2024, and 17% of recent quitters cited changes to work arrangements.McKinsey American Opportunity Survey, May 2025
71% of US teleworkers say working from home helps their work-life balance.Pew Research, 2023
98% of remote workers want to work remotely at least part of the time for the rest of their careers.Buffer State of Remote Work 2023
None of the 41 US and Canadian four-day-week pilot companies returned to five days.4 Day Week Global
28% of working adults in Great Britain work hybrid — 45% of those earning £50k+ versus 8% of those under £20k.ONS, Jun 2025
9% of employed people in the EU usually work from home, up from 5.5% in 2019; Finland leads at 20.5%.Eurostat 2025 data, via Remote Work Europe

Figures were checked against the original pages in October 2026. Where the original was unavailable, the secondary source is named. Spotted something out of date? Let us know.
